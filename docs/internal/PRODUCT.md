# Podframes

Podframes is a creator workspace for producing repeatable two-host podcast videos. Creators start with a topic, source links, or a pasted Markdown/text script, then direct characters, voices, delivery, supporting visuals, captions, and export. Saved episodes serve as templates for consistent cast and visual identity.

The primary surface is the studio. The central action is making and reviewing an episode, with a persistent video preview and clear progress through cast, script, videos, editing, and export. The library supports resuming work and creating an episode from a template. The landing page introduces this workflow.

Creators need readable multilingual scripts, clear paid-generation actions, visible errors and progress, and control over duration and delivery. The interface should feel like a bold, expressive media studio. Jellypod is the confirmed reference. Follow the computer light/dark preference. The approved direction uses mint accents, warm neutral surfaces, confident sans typography, rounded controls, generous dialogue rows, character imagery, and a dark preview.
